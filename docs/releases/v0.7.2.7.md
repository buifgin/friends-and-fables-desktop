# Friends & Fables Desktop 0.7.2.7

Release date: 2026-10-12

This release brings settings together, adds more appearance and dice controls, and improves the desktop toolbar and campaign panels.

- Open Settings from the desktop gear to manage Campaign, Appearance, Translation, Music, Combat, and General settings in one place. Music settings are reserved for future options.
- Customize the campaign background, panel opacity and borders, button colors, spacing, and transparency across the campaign controls. Favorites use a compact two-column layout and can be grouped with selected checks.
- Correct roll results with ability, modifier, and proficiency adjustments. Advantage applies to the current roll only; category and character or campaign defaults remain separate. Save, delete, and overwrite defaults from the roll controls.
- Attack-roll spells use the attack correction flow without changing damage rolls. Right-side campaign controls can be hidden and restored through native controls.
- The music header shortcut sits beside playback controls and before the avatar row, with a circular close control and settings in the footer. The header supports up to six avatars.
- Long names wrap more cleanly, labels align better, and the right panel, map, and gutter handle scrolling and layout more reliably.
- Use Alt+Enter or F11 for fullscreen and Ctrl+Shift+B to show or hide the desktop top bar. A General setting can keep the top bar hidden in fullscreen.
- Combat settings offer two campaign-local options, both off by default: automatically accept the native damage prompt after your own confirmed hit, and optionally hide only that hit's linked intermediate Franz response. Automation handles one attack against one target; batches remain manual, misses are not automated, and critical damage follows the native prompt. Hiding the intermediate response does not stop its generation or affect the final response or unrelated messages.
- Startup and window lifecycle handling is more reliable.

Windows includes the English–Russian translation model. Linux machine translation continues to use a separate local LibreTranslate service. Downloads are Linux x86_64 AppImage and Windows x64 portable EXE, with SHA256SUMS. Windows builds remain unsigned; automatic updates are not included.
