# Friends & Fables Desktop 0.6.0

Release date: 2026-10-05

This release builds on 0.5.2 with improvements to music, translation, appearance, and window controls.

- Music controls expand with an animation, keep a shortcut to the music library, and omit the toolbar Stop button.
- Translation fixes cover damage and dice labels, glossary casing, world and campaign names, and descriptions with a single loading indicator. Progression text is translated in order.
- Appearance dialogs have centered close controls, including Saved Instructions opened with `/sp`. Theme borders and blur are refined for dice panels, action pickers, and the selected tab.
- On Hyprland, minimizing hides the app to the tray; selecting the tray icon or Show app restores it.
- The fullscreen control updates its button state to match the window.

Progression paragraph formatting and curated class translation remain future work.
